--- layout: archive title: "Research activities" permalink: /portfolio/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} My research connects high-resolution spatial data with the conservation and inclusive use of cultural heritage. The work ranges from field survey design to point-cloud processing, GIS analysis and interoperable 3D information models. ## Methods and applications
Survey and reality capture
Terrestrial laser scanning, close-range and UAS photogrammetry, mobile mapping, GNSS and total stations.
3D information models
Scan-to-BIM, HBIM and digital twins for documentation, conservation and management.
Spatial analysis for inclusion
Data collection and modelling for pedestrian accessibility in complex historic settings.
## Ongoing and recent projects ### ACROSS - Accessible historic urban contexts (2023-2026) Member of the PRIN 2022 research team developing static and dynamic databases for accessibility in historic urban areas. The work combines mobile mapping, machine-learning point-cloud processing, spatial information extraction, thematic mapping and WebGIS. ### HYMantovalley (2023-present) Research team member in the I3 Programme Applied Research Hub. Activities include 3D environmental monitoring with UAS photogrammetry, LiDAR and thermal sensors, and the integration of the resulting data in GIS/BIM environments supporting a sustainable masterplan. ### HBIM and digital conservation Research on integrated survey, automated modelling and information management for complex heritage sites, including the Holy Sepulchre Basilica in Jerusalem, the Arch of Augustus in Aosta, and historic buildings and urban environments in Mantua, Venice and Sabbioneta. ## Projects and case studies {% for post in site.portfolio %} {% include archive-single.html %} {% endfor %}
